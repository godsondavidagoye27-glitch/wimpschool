# WimpSchool Launch Checklist

## Priority 0 — Must complete before full launch
- [ ] Harden Supabase roles and permissions for admin, teacher, parent, and super-admin access.
- [ ] Test the full payment lifecycle end to end with real or sandbox transactions.
- [ ] Add clear error states and recovery guidance for failed logins, invites, and payments.
- [ ] Create operational support and legal pages for privacy, terms, and help.
- [ ] Validate backup and restore readiness for school records and payments.
- [ ] Confirm notifications and invite delivery work reliably in production settings.
- [ ] Review audit trails for fee changes, payment updates, and school settings.
- [ ] Perform real-user testing for admin, teacher, parent, and super-admin flows.
- [ ] Prepare support contacts, onboarding steps, and a launch runbook.
- [ ] Verify performance and mobile experience on low-bandwidth devices.

## Priority 1 — Strongly recommended
- [ ] Add confirmation emails and receipts for fee payments.
- [ ] Improve reporting exports and downloadable summaries.
- [ ] Add a simple school onboarding guide for first-time admins.
- [ ] Add a maintenance and outage notice workflow.

## Priority 2 — Nice to have
- [ ] Add dark mode and additional branding controls.
- [ ] Add offline-friendly caching for repeated parent portal visits.
- [ ] Add more automation for attendance reminders and fee reminders.
